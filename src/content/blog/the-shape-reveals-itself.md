I landed in San Diego around midday and went straight into conversations. Good ones. Now it's evening. I'm taking a little time to contemplate how I've been approaching the past couple of weeks.

There's real architecture under this trip. A structure I've turned over from a lot of angles.

And I still have no idea what tomorrow actually looks like.

That gap used to be the uncomfortable part.

Tonight it's the interesting part.

You can live on an "edge" where everything runs hour by hour and day by day. Most descriptions of that edge make it sound like survival. Braced. Reactive. Waiting for the next thing to come at you.

This is a different edge.

It's the edge of a puzzle.

Read something as a problem and a particular world assembles around you. Something broke. Somebody should've caught it. The work ahead is repair. Repair returns you to where you already were. Read the identical facts as a [puzzle](https://www.infinitegameos.io/concepts/puzzles-not-problems) and the work becomes finding the thing that was placed there, where the solving is the actual point.

Nothing about the week changed. The posture walking in did.

An earlier version of me would've arrived with the shape already (fully) drawn.

He'd have had a picture in his head of how this ought to go. Then he'd have spent the week (consciously or unconsciously) steering toward it. Shaping the conversations. Reading the room for openings toward a thing already decided.

Strategy has its place. I use it constantly. This is a different grip.

What I brought this time is the intention and the principle. What I left at home is the (rigid) form.

The form gets to reveal itself. Through the conversations, through the inquiry, through the parts where somebody says the thing I hadn't considered. You can't shape something that doesn't want to be shaped that way. Push it and you spend the whole week pushing.

What makes that available instead of frightening comes down to one line.

I'm genuinely okay if it doesn't work out.

Not performing okay. Actually okay. That's the hinge the whole thing turns on. Nonchalance stops being a pose the moment you've made real peace with the part you don't control.

Something I didn't expect: walking in without a crystallized answer is what builds the trust.

When the structure gets discovered together, through the alignment and the coherence and the actual back and forth, everyone is inside it. I get to be curious about what someone actually wants. What their purpose is. What fills their cup. What lets them do work that means something while being beautifully compensated for it.

That's a [structure everyone can feel good standing in](https://www.infinitegameos.io/concepts/positive-sum-by-design). You don't hand somebody one of those. You find it with them.

It's a different octave of [beginner's mind](https://www.lanebelone.com/blog/f/nobodys-good-at-it-yet). Setting down the one who knows how the old way is done. Coming in fluid instead.

The principle underneath stays solid. Hardened, in a good way. Dependable, consistent, something you can lean your weight on.

The route to it is what stays loose.

Sometimes you know the principle. It's just there. The specifics run off it.

Sometimes you don't. Sometimes you move through the detailed strategy and the specific conversations to find out what the principle underneath even is.

Both directions are the dance.

Wherever your clarity actually sits, start there. If what you've got is a detailed plan, work it and watch for the principle it keeps pointing at. If what you've got is a principle you know in your body, run the specifics off it.

I'm excited to see how this week goes, because I have no idea what will happen lol.

If your own week looks something like that, the smallest thing available is the word you're using for it. [Puzzle rather than problem](https://www.infinitegameos.io/concepts/puzzles-not-problems). Same facts, different person walking in.
