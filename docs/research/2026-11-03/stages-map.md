## Patterns in what these hosts look for

**A lived story with one sharp angle.** Reboot wants a guest to "talk with Jerry about your specific challenge" (https://www.reboot.io/podcast/sign-up/). Meaningful Work, Meaningful Life asks for "a clear purpose", "honest lessons" and "depth over hype" (https://francinebeleyi.com/mwmls11). Lesser Known Operators wants retired SOF with "unique, untold stories" (https://pitchpodcasts.com/pods/lesser-known-operators/311955, aggregator). The Fun Question books "Escape Rooms" or "Silent Disco", never "fun" in general.

**Something you can show.** How I AI asks guests to live-demo one or two use cases in about 30 minutes (https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/announcing-a-brand-new-podcast-how). Professor Game wants "real-world results" (https://talks.co/professor-game-podcast). Creator Science guests lead with numbers. AI newsletters want workflows, data and diagrams (https://www.ai-supremacy.com/p/guest-posts-on-ai-supreamcy).

**An idea over a biography on big stages.** TED tells TEDx speakers to pitch "the specific idea and your work on it, not just your personal story" (https://www.ted.com/about/programs-initiatives/tedx-program/speaking-at-tedx). The Infinite Player framework leads there, and the Green Beret story supports it.

**Proof you can hold a room.** Esalen wants a workshop already "attended by at least 20 participants" (https://www.esalen.org/visit/teach). A-Fest favours speakers "who are established or can share links to previous presentations." Kripalu weighs "capacity to promote their work successfully" (https://kripalu.org/program-proposals).

**Teaching, never selling.** Nomad Cruise: "Sales pitches for your business are not welcome" (https://www.nomadcruise.com/speaker). Tiny Buddha and Ness Labs reject self-promotion in the body. Most community and nomad stages pay nothing.

**A book opens the bigger rooms.** The upper-mid and stretch leadership shows (Coaching for Leaders, The Double Win, Game Changers, The One You Feed) mostly book published authors (inference from episode lists). "Unleash Your Humble Alpha" is Lane's ticket there.

**Relationships before forms.** Only a minority of shows publish a guest route. On Substack, guest slots and Lives usually follow Notes, recommendations and earlier conversations (inference). Beyond The Prompt, Infinite Loops, Wisdom 2.0 and the Jedburgh Podcast all point to warm introductions (inference).

**Lane's military story is an entry ticket. His Infinite Game frame is what makes him memorable.** Veteran shows book the "Green Beret to entrepreneur" arc often (Security Halt, VET S.O.S., Lesser Known Operators). None of them has run a Carse conversation (inference from the episode lists read).

## Limits of this map

- Apple Podcasts shows month and day without the year for current-year episodes. Rows that say "(2026, inference)" rest on feed order.
- Some dates and routes come from directories (Podwise, Audible, Listen Notes, Talks.co, PitchPodcasts) because the official page blocked reads. Each row names its source.
- Guest routes behind a login (Talks.co, PitchPodcasts, Starter Story) were not opened. No accounts were created.
- Rating counts are a rough proxy for size. Download figures are almost never published.
